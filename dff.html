DUMBO
Film Festival

The Dumbo Film Festival (DFF) is an annual independent film festival based in DUMBO, Brooklyn, spotlighting emerging filmmakers from around the world.

The refresh reimagines DFF’s identity through the visual language of DUMBO, drawing from its industrial architecture, creative culture, and cinematic character to build a bold, distinctly Brooklyn presence.
